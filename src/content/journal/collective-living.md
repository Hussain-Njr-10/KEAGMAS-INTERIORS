The concept of the home is shifting. We are seeing a move towards collective living models where private sanctuaries are balanced with expansive, shared amenity spaces. 

At KEAGMAS INTERIORS, we approach these hybrid residential projects by clearly defining public and private thresholds. A change in floor material or a subtle drop in ceiling height can subconsciously guide inhabitants from social engagement to private retreat, creating a harmonious living ecosystem.
