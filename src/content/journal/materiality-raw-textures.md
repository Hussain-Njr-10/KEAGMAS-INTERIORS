After a decade of hyper-polished, synthetic finishes dominating the luxury market, we are witnessing a profound return to raw, honest materiality. 

Unfilled travertine, deeply grained oak, and patinated brass are materials that record time. They age gracefully, acquiring a patina that tells the story of the people who interact with them. This is the essence of true luxury: spaces that improve, rather than degrade, with the passage of time.
